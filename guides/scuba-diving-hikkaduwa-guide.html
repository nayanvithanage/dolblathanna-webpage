What the course actually involves

This is a genuine, internationally-recognised PADI Open Water Diver certification — not a single guided dive, but a real multi-day course covering the skills and safety procedures you need to dive independently afterward, anywhere in the world. Expect real time commitment, not an afternoon activity.

Best time to go

November through April, during the dry season, gives the calmest seas and best visibility for diving. If diving is a real priority for your trip, it's worth factoring into when you travel, not just what you do once you're here.
