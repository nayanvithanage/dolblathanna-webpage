Where to Stay

Resorts on the South Coast

South Coast, Sri Lanka • 3 min read

A resort here means a larger property with the full set of on-site amenities as standard — pools, spas, restaurants, sometimes a private beach. All three we list sit in and around Tangalle, an hour east of Ahangama, where quieter, wilder beaches make the resort format make sense — real space to build a bigger property without crowding a busy stretch of coast.

Already know you want this? See our real, verified resorts in Tangalle, with real ratings and direct booking links.

What to actually expect

Arinna Beach Resort is a genuinely right-on-the-beach property with a private beach and pool, breakfast included. Chaya Beach Hotel is a 2-minute walk to Tangalle Beach with a garden and hot tub, AC rooms, breakfast included. Lankavatara Ocean Retreat & Spa, near Kalametiya just outside central Tangalle, is the largest of the three — two outdoor pools and a full spa, beachfront. All three trade on having everything in one place, so you're not arranging transport for a meal or a spa treatment.

Who this suits

Travellers who'd rather not leave the property much — everything you need is on-site — and anyone wanting the reliability of a bigger operation over the individual character of a smaller stay. If you want more privacy and less shared facility use, a villa in the same area gets you space without the resort scale.

What we checked before listing one

  • Real reviews. All three properties here have genuine Booking.com and Agoda listings behind them, and Lankavatara also has a real Klook listing.
  • Actually beachfront, not just nearby. We checked real walking distance or direct beach access for each — "beach resort" doesn't always mean what it says.
  • Real, current amenities. Pool count, spa facilities, and what's included in the room rate vary — check the specific listing rather than assuming resort-standard across the board.

Tangalle's quieter coastline is the right setting for this style of stay — if you want the resort format closer to Ahangama's surf towns, you won't find it; this coast's resorts are specifically out here.
