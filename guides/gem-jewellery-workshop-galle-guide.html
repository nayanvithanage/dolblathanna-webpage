Gem & Jewellery Workshop (Galle)

South Coast, Sri Lanka • 4 min read

Sri Lanka has been known for its gemstones for centuries, and Galle has a long tradition of cutting and jewellery making. A workshop visit lets you see how a rough stone becomes a finished piece, which is far more interesting than a shop that simply wants to sell you one.

What the experience involves

In our research we found a traditional gems and jewellery workshop experience in Galle running about three and a half hours, with a strong review record on GetYourGuide. A typical visit includes seeing stones cut and polished and learning how to tell quality from imitation.

Buying advice

Gems are the one place on this coast where it genuinely pays to be careful. Be wary of anyone steering you to a shop you did not choose, of pressure to buy on the spot, and of "investment" claims. If you do buy, ask for a written description of the stone, and be sceptical of unusually low prices.

Who it suits

People curious about craft, and anyone planning to buy jewellery who would rather learn what they are looking at first. It pairs naturally with a day in Galle Fort.

Practical tips

  • Look at the process first and decide about buying afterwards.
  • Compare prices at more than one place before buying anything expensive.
  • Never feel obliged to buy to be polite.
