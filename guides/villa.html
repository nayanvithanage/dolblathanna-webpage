What to actually expect

Ziba Luxury Villa is an intimate seven-room property a 9-minute walk from Goyambokka Beach in Tangalle, with its own infinity pool and sea-view rooms — a quieter, design-led option away from the busier beach towns. Azure Mirissa (listed under its full name, Azure Resort Mirissa) is a 5-minute walk from Mirissa Beach with garden and balcony rooms, genuine private parking, and a more relaxed pace than a resort of the same size would offer.

Who this suits

Groups and families wanting real space to spread out, couples wanting genuine privacy over shared resort facilities, and anyone who'd rather have a pool that isn't shared with other guests. If you actually want the shared bar, restaurant, and activity desk that come with a bigger property, a resort will suit you better.

What we checked before listing one

  • Real reviews. Both properties here have genuine Booking.com and Agoda listings behind them.
  • Actually villa-scale, not a small resort with the word "villa" in its name. We checked room count and layout — both listed here are genuinely small, self-contained properties.
  • Real distance to the beach. Both are a specific, short walk from real sand, not a vague "coastal" location.

Tangalle's quieter, wilder beaches and Mirissa's more central position give you two genuinely different bases if privacy and space are what you're after.
