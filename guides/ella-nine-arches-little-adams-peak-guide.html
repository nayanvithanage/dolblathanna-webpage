Ella Day Tour Guide

Ella, hill country • 3 min read

Ella is one of Sri Lanka's most photographed hill-country towns, and this day tour covers its real highlights in one guided trip: the Nine Arch Bridge, a mini train ride, Udawalawe Elephant Orphanage, Rawana Falls, and a climb to Little Adam's Peak.

What the day actually covers

  • Nine Arch Bridge. Ella's famous colonial-era railway viaduct, a real architectural and photography highlight.
  • A mini train ride. A short taste of Sri Lanka's scenic rail network.
  • Udawalawe Elephant Orphanage. A wildlife stop distinct from a safari — worth knowing this isn't the Udawalawe National Park safari itself.
  • Rawana Falls. A waterfall stop along the route.
  • Little Adam's Peak. A real climb with hill-country views — bring proper footwear.

Worth knowing: pickup is offered from a wide range of locations across Sri Lanka, not just Ella itself — check the booking page for the option closest to where you're staying.
