Sunset Catamaran Sailing + Dinner

South Coast, Sri Lanka • 3 min read

A sunset on the water is one of the simplest ways to end a day on this coast. Out of Mirissa, there are catamaran sailings timed around the evening light, some of them with dinner on board, which turns it from a short cruise into a full evening.

Looking to book? See where this stands and the closest bookable option.

What we found

In our research we found a sunset catamaran sailing with dinner included, listed on GetYourGuide for Mirissa. We did not find the same listing on a platform we use, so we cannot link to it directly.

A bookable alternative

What we can link to is a private sunset cruise from Mirissa, about three hours, with the option to snorkel or paddleboard and with light snacks and a drink. It is not a sit-down dinner sail, but it is the same sunset on the same water, and it is a real listing with a booking link. See our private sunset cruise page.

Planning notes

Sailings depend on the sea and the season, and evening departures can be cancelled in rough conditions, so keep a spare evening free if you can.

Practical tips

  • Check the departure time against the actual sunset time for your dates.
  • Bring a light layer, since it cools on the water once the sun is down.
  • Ask about the cancellation policy for rough weather.
