Deep Sea / Game Fishing

South Coast, Sri Lanka • 3 min read

The deep water off this coast supports offshore fishing, and Mirissa and Weligama are the usual departure points. It is a niche activity, more for people who already fish than for casual visitors.

Looking to book? See where this stands and the closest bookable option.

How it is booked

In our research we found multiple independent operators, most of whom take bookings directly by message rather than through a booking platform. That means prices and availability vary, so ask more than one operator.

What to ask

The boat size and safety equipment, trip length, whether gear and bait are provided, what happens to the catch, and the cancellation policy for bad weather. Agree the price and what is included in advance.

Season and conditions

The sea is rougher in the southwest monsoon months, and many trips do not run then. Ask about conditions on the day, and take seasickness tablets if you are prone to it.

Practical tips

  • Confirm the price and inclusions in writing.
  • Check the boat has life jackets and radio.
  • Bring sun protection, water, and a hat.
