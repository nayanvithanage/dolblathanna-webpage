Surf Camps on the South Coast

South Coast, Sri Lanka • 3 min read
Who this suits
SurfersSolo travellers

Weligama Bay's wide, beginner-friendly break has made it the south coast's surf-camp capital — bunk rooms, board hire, and lessons bundled right into the stay itself. If surfing is the actual reason for the trip, this is the category built specifically around that.

Already know you want this? See our real, verified surf camps in Weligama, with real ratings and direct booking links.

What to actually expect

Kickin' Surf Lodge is a dedicated surf lodge on Weligama's beach strip, built around board hire and lessons on the bay's famously gentle beginner break — a 6-minute walk from the water. OASIA — Boutique Surf House takes a quieter, more design-led approach: an adults-only property with a year-round outdoor pool, further from the break (about 18 minutes on foot) but a step up in comfort for couples who still want to be near the water. Both bundle the surf-town lifestyle into the stay, not just a room to sleep in between sessions.

Who this suits

Beginners taking their first lessons on Weligama's forgiving break, and anyone who wants the social, board-and-lesson-included version of a beach stay rather than sorting equipment and instruction separately. If you'd rather stay somewhere quiet and book lessons independently, a guest house in Weligama gives you more flexibility; see our Weligama surf guide for more on the town itself.

What we checked before listing one

  • Real reviews. Both properties here have genuine Booking.com and Agoda listings behind them.
  • Actual proximity to the break. We note the real walk time to Weligama Beach for each — "near the surf" can mean very different distances.
  • What's genuinely on-site. Board hire, lessons, and shared spaces vary property to property — check the specific listing for what's actually included versus what's arranged separately.

Weligama's break is one of the most beginner-friendly in Sri Lanka, and staying right on it is the easiest way to actually get in the water every day of the trip.
