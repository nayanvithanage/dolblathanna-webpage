Snorkeling (Weligama)

South Coast, Sri Lanka • 3 min read
Who this suits
Wildlife and nature loversSurfersBudget travellers

Weligama Bay is best known for surf, but it also has reef and shallow water suited to snorkeling, with a PADI dive centre based on the bay that also runs snorkeling.

Looking to book? See where this stands and the closest bookable option.

What to expect

Shore-based snorkeling in the bay is a relaxed, low-key activity, with visibility that depends on the sea state and season. It suits people who want to try snorkeling without a long boat trip.

If turtles are the goal

Mirissa, a short distance along the coast, is where the well-known turtle snorkeling trips run. Our Mirissa snorkeling page has the real bookable options.

Safety

Check conditions with the operator before you get in, wear a flotation vest if you are not a confident swimmer, and avoid touching coral or wildlife.

Practical tips

  • Go in calmer months and mornings for the best visibility.
  • Use reef-safe sunscreen.
  • Ask whether equipment is provided.
