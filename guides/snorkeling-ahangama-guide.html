Snorkeling (Ahangama)

South Coast, Sri Lanka • 3 min read
Who this suits
Wildlife and nature loversSurfersBudget travellers

Ahangama is better known for its surf breaks than its snorkeling, but there are options nearby for a morning in the water, and a more established snorkeling scene a short distance along the coast.

Looking to book? See where this stands and the closest bookable option.

What is available

In our research we found a snorkeling operator based in Ahangama, and another that runs trips towards Unawatuna. Our day trip guide also mentions Snake Island as a calm-day snorkeling spot. Availability and conditions vary, so check locally.

For a reliable trip

If you want a dependable, bookable snorkeling trip, Mirissa has the better-known option, with turtles. See our Mirissa snorkeling page.

Safety

This coast has strong currents and swell at times. Take local advice before going in, and snorkel with an operator rather than alone when conditions are uncertain.

Practical tips

  • Ask your guesthouse for current sea conditions.
  • Choose calmer days and mornings.
  • Never snorkel alone in swell.
