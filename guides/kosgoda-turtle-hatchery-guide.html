Kosgoda Turtle Hatchery Guide

Kosgoda, near Bentota • 3 min read

Kosgoda's turtle hatchery sits a short drive up the coast from Ahangama, near Bentota — a real conservation site, not a pure tourist attraction. The work here is protecting sea turtle eggs from poachers, raising hatching success rates, and releasing hatchlings back into the ocean. It's a genuinely worthwhile stop if you're already covering the Galle area, rather than a destination in its own right for most visitors.

What the visit actually involves

Expect a guided look at how the hatchery protects eggs and raises hatchlings before release — a hands-on conservation visit rather than a display or photo-only stop. The tour also includes a stop at a gem/jewellery workshop, a common pairing on this stretch of coast given the region's gem-trading history.

Is it worth the trip?

If sea turtle conservation genuinely interests you, or you're combining it with a Galle Fort day (it's close enough to make a realistic pairing), yes. If you're purely after a beach-and-relax day, it's a reasonable add-on rather than a must-do — treat it as a worthwhile extra on a day you're already spending exploring the coast north of Ahangama.
