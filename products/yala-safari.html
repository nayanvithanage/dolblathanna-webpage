Yala National Park Jeep Safari

One of the highest leopard densities anywhere on earth, plus elephants, sloth bears, and 200+ bird species — a dawn or afternoon jeep safari through Sri Lanka's most famous national park.

What's Included

  • Private 4x4 safari jeep with driver-guide
  • Park entry ticket (foreign visitor rate)
  • Dawn or afternoon game-drive slot — the two best wildlife windows
  • Pickup coordination from the South Coast on request

What to Expect

You'll ride in an open-sided jeep with a driver-guide who knows the park's tracks and where recent sightings have happened. Yala is famous above all for leopards — it has one of the highest leopard densities recorded anywhere — but a typical safari also turns up elephants, crocodiles, buffalo, and a huge range of birdlife even on a quiet leopard day. Dawn entries (jeeps line up before the gates open) generally give the best combination of light, cooler temperatures, and active wildlife.
