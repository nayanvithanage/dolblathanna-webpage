What to Expect

Sinharaja is Sri Lanka's last significant area of primary rainforest and a UNESCO World Heritage Site — a genuinely different landscape from anything else on this stretch of coast. This is a real forest hike with an experienced guide, not a paved nature-walk experience, so go in expecting proper trekking conditions: uneven ground, humidity, and a slower pace than a beach day. It's a full-day commitment once you factor in transport from Mirissa, so plan around it rather than squeezing it into a half-day.
