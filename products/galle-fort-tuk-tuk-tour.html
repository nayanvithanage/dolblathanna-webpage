Galle City Sightseeing Tour by Tuk Tuk

An open-air tuk-tuk loop through Galle Fort's ramparts and streets, plus the stilt fishermen, Koggala Lake, and a jewellery workshop stop — a wide-angle look at the area beyond the Fort walls alone.

From (indicative)~$20–35 USD / person Check Prices & Book

What's Included

  • Hotel pickup and open-air tuk-tuk for the full loop
  • Galle Dutch Fort — ramparts, old streets, UNESCO-listed old town
  • Stilt fishermen viewing stop along the coast
  • Koggala Lake boat safari to Cinnamon Island
  • Stops at a Hindu temple, Kaluwella Christian Church, the Dutch Market, an Ayurvedic centre, and a jewellery workshop
  • Roughly 4 hours total

What to Expect

This is a wider loop than a Galle Fort walk alone — the tuk-tuk format means you cover more ground than you would on foot, taking in the Fort itself plus several real South Coast landmarks in one outing: the stilt fishermen's traditional coastal fishing method, a boat ride on Koggala Lake, and a couple of craft/culture stops along the way. It suits a visitor who wants a broad first taste of the area rather than a slow, dedicated Fort walk — if you'd rather go deep on just the Fort, our Galle Fort guide covers that version instead.
