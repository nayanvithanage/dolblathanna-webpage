Mirissa Snorkeling Boat Trip — Shared Group

The main, most direct way to snorkel with sea turtles off Mirissa — a shared boat to shallow water where green and hawksbill turtles feed close to the surface.

From (indicative)~$25–40 USD / person Check Prices & Book

What's Included

  • Boat transfer from Mirissa harbour to the turtle feeding area
  • Mask, snorkel, and life jacket
  • Guide/boat crew who know where turtles are feeding that day
  • Typically 1–2 hours total, including time in the water

What to Expect

This is a near-shore trip, not an open-water excursion — the water is shallow enough that turtles come up close to graze on seagrass, and most trips get a real, unhurried look rather than a distant glimpse. No swimming experience beyond basic comfort in open water is needed, and a life jacket is standard if you'd rather float than tread water.
