What to Expect

Kosgoda's hatchery works to protect sea turtle eggs from poachers, raise hatching rates, and improve survival odds before releasing hatchlings back to the ocean — a real, hands-on look at a conservation effort rather than a pure sightseeing stop. It sits a short distance up the coast from Ahangama, near Bentota, which makes it a realistic add-on to a Galle-area day rather than its own dedicated trip for most visitors.
