What to Expect

Galle Fort is a genuinely living neighbourhood, not a museum piece — cafes, guesthouses, and shops still run from inside the same Dutch-built walls that have stood since the 17th century. If your time in the fort is limited to a half-day, a booked activity takes the guesswork out of what to prioritise: a guided walk covers the ramparts, the lighthouse, and the old grid of streets in one go, while a boat tour or a tea-plantation trip gives you a reason to venture further along the coast beyond the walls themselves.
