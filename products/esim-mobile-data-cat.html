What to Expect

Sri Lanka's airport SIM counters are slow, cash-preferring, and one more queue after a long flight. An eSIM sidesteps all of it — install the profile before you leave home, and it activates the moment you land and connect to a local tower. Mobitel's unlimited plans suit anyone streaming maps or video for longer stretches; Hutch's smaller data-plus-call bundles suit lighter use or a shorter trip. Either way, you keep your own number active for calls and texts from home, with a separate data line just for local use.
