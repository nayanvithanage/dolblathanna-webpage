Mirissa Whale Watching — Shared Boat

What's Included

  • Return boat trip from Mirissa harbour, typically 2–3 hours
  • Shared group boat (not a private charter)
  • Licensed skipper and crew, life jackets
  • Early-morning departure slot (best sighting conditions)

What to Expect

This is the standard, most direct way to do a Mirissa whale watching trip — a shared boat departing early (around 6–6:30am), when calmer water and quieter conditions generally mean better sighting odds than a late-morning departure. Blue whales are the headline draw, with sperm whales, spinner dolphins, and the occasional Bryde's whale also possible. Sharing the boat with other visitors keeps the price down compared to a private charter, and is the right choice if sightings matter more to you than privacy.
