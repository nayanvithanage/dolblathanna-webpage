What to Expect

Koggala Lake is a real change of pace from the South Coast's beaches and surf towns — a slow boat ride through mangrove channels and small islands, with a stop at Cinnamon Island to see how cinnamon bark is traditionally peeled and processed. It's a low-effort, high-reward addition to a Galle-area day: no hiking, no early start, just a couple of calm hours on the water.
