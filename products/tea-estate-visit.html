What to Expect

Handunugoda is genuinely close to Ahangama, which makes it a rare thing on this coast: a real tea-estate experience without the multi-hour drive up into the hill country. Virgin White Tea is the estate's signature product — a rarer, less-processed style than standard black tea. Visits here are typically a low-cost or free walk-in rather than a pre-booked ticketed experience, so treat the price above as indicative and confirm current access directly.
