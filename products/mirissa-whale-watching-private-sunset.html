Honest Note on Timing

Worth knowing upfront: the early-morning departure (around 6–6:30am) is generally considered the better window for actually sighting whales, since calmer water and quieter conditions in the early hours tend to improve sighting odds compared to later in the day. A sunset trip trades some of that sighting probability for a private boat and a scenic golden-hour setting on the water — it's a genuinely different experience, not simply the same whale-watching trip at a different time. Dolphin pods are still a realistic sight regardless of timing.

Who This Is For

Choose this over the shared boat if privacy, a flexible pace, and a sunset setting matter more to you than maximizing whale-sighting odds — for example, a couple or small group wanting their own boat rather than sharing with other visitors. If seeing a whale is the priority, the early shared boat is the better-odds choice.
