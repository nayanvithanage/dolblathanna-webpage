What to Expect

This is the most direct Yala option for anyone already based on the South Coast — pickup and drop-off right from Mirissa, no need to arrange your own transport to a separate starting point. As a shared-group safari, you'll ride with other visitors in the same jeep, which keeps the price down compared to a private option. Yala is best known for its leopard density, but elephants, crocodiles, buffalo, and strong birdlife round out most drives even on a quieter leopard day.
