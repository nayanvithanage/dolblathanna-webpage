What to Look For in a Boat

Boat Size

Under 20–25 Passengers

Smaller boats give a better view and tend toward a more responsible approach to the whales themselves — worth checking before you book.

Departure Time

Early Boats, 6–6:30am

Generally calmer water and better sighting odds than late-morning departures — an early alarm is worth it here.

Season

November – April

Matches the dry season and calmer seas. December–March is the strongest window; most operators pause outside this range entirely.
