Mirissa Whale Watching Tour

One of the most reliable places on earth to see blue whales from a boat, plus sperm whales, spinner dolphins, and the occasional Bryde's whale — a 2–3 hour trip out from Mirissa harbour.

What to Expect

You'll head out from Mirissa harbour before sunrise, since early departures generally get calmer water and better sighting odds than late-morning boats. Blue whales are the headline draw — Mirissa sits near a deep continental shelf where they feed close enough to shore for a realistic day trip, and sightings during peak season (December–March) run well above what most whale watching destinations worldwide can offer. Nothing in nature is guaranteed, but dolphin pods turn up often enough that most trips see real marine life even on a quieter whale day.

What to Look For in a Boat

Boat Size

Under 20–25 Passengers

Smaller boats give a better view and tend toward a more responsible approach to the whales themselves — worth checking before you book.

Departure Time

Early Boats, 6–6:30am

Generally calmer water and better sighting odds than late-morning departures — an early alarm is worth it here.

Season

November – April

Matches the dry season and calmer seas. December–March is the strongest window; most operators pause outside this range entirely.
