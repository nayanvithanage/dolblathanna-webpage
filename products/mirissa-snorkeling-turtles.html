Mirissa Snorkeling with Turtles

A short boat ride out from Mirissa harbour to a real, reliable spot where green and hawksbill turtles feed close to the surface — no dive certification needed, just a mask and a willingness to get in the water.

From (indicative)~$25–40 USD / person Check Prices & Book

What's Included

  • Boat transfer from Mirissa harbour to the turtle feeding area
  • Mask, snorkel, and life jacket
  • Guide/boat crew who know where turtles are reliably feeding that day
  • Typically 1–2 hours total, including time in the water

What to Expect

This is a near-shore trip, not an open-water excursion — the water is shallow enough that turtles come up close to graze on seagrass, and most trips get a real, unhurried look rather than a distant glimpse. Green turtles are the most commonly seen, with hawksbills turning up often enough that most trips see more than one individual. No swimming experience beyond basic comfort in open water is needed, and a life jacket is standard if you'd rather float than tread water. Visibility is generally better earlier in the day, before boat traffic picks up.

Worth Knowing Before You Go

No Diving Needed

Surface-Level Only

Turtles feed close enough to the surface that snorkeling is genuinely enough — no scuba certification or gear required.

Keep Your Distance

Don't Touch or Chase

Good operators brief you on not touching or crowding the turtles — it's better for the animals and usually gets you a calmer, longer look anyway.
