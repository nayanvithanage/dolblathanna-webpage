Weligama Surf Lessons

The single best place on the South Coast to learn — a wide, sandy, gently sloping bay with small, forgiving waves almost year-round, and the coast's highest concentration of surf schools.

  • Beach-based basics before paddling out
  • Typically 1.5–2 hours in the water

What to Expect

Weligama Bay's shape is what makes it work for beginners: a broad, sandy-bottomed break with a gentle, rolling wave that rarely gets big or punchy, unlike the reef breaks toward Ahangama and Midigama. Good schools keep a low student-to-instructor ratio (four or fewer per instructor for true beginners) and spend real time on beach basics before you paddle out — that grounding is what separates a genuinely useful first lesson from a rushed one. Expect a busy line-up, especially mid-morning; an early or late session is worth it if you'd rather avoid the crowds.
