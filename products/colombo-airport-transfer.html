Airport & Transfers

Colombo Airport Transfer

A private driver from Bandaranaike International Airport straight to the South Coast — around 2.5–3 hours depending on traffic, booked and priced before you land instead of negotiated on arrival.

Fixed price~$180–210 USD / vehicle Check Prices & Book

What's Included

  • Private vehicle and driver, no ride-sharing with other passengers
  • Direct pickup from the airport arrivals area
  • Drop-off anywhere along the South Coast (Ahangama, Weligama, Galle, Mirissa)
  • Fixed price agreed before you fly, not negotiated on arrival

What to Expect

Most visitors arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport after a long flight, and the last thing you want at that point is to negotiate a fare with a queue of touts. A pre-booked private transfer means a driver is waiting with your name, the price is already agreed, and you can sleep or watch the coastline go by for the 2.5–3 hour drive south rather than manage logistics. It's a genuine door-to-door option — no changing trains, no working out which bus goes where with jet lag setting in.
